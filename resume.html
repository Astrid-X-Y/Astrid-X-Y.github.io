Selected Research & Academic Projects

Glyphosate Exposure and Depression Outcomes in U.S. Adults

Group Research Project | April 2026 - June 2026

Investigated the association between glyphosate exposure and depression outcomes using data from the 2017-2018 National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES).

Research activities included data cleaning, variable construction, and statistical analysis using R. Reproducible data processing workflows were developed to manage biomonitoring and health-related variables, while multivariable logistic regression models were applied to evaluate exposure-outcome associations and estimate adjusted odds ratios.

The analysis incorporated epidemiologic considerations related to confounding, model specification, and the interpretation of observational associations between environmental chemical exposure and mental health outcomes.
